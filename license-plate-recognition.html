License-Plate Recognition

Computer visionYOLOv8PaddleOCR

Developed an intelligent Automated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) System that combines YOLOv8 object detection and PaddleOCR text recognition to automatically detect, extract, and recognize vehicle license plate numbers from images and video streams. The system leverages YOLOv8 for accurate and efficient license plate localization, while PaddleOCR processes the detected regions to extract alphanumeric characters and convert them into readable text.

By integrating deep learning and optical character recognition, the project streamlines vehicle identification, reduces manual plate verification, and demonstrates the practical application of computer vision in intelligent transportation, automated parking management, and security monitoring systems.
